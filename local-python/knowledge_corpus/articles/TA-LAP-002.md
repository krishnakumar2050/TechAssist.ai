# Laptop fails POST after physical damage

ID: TA-LAP-002 | Platform: Dell laptops | Category: hardware / motherboard

Symptom: No normal startup after a drop or spill
Possible causes (not confirmed): Physical damage
Resolution:
1. Record what happened
2. Stop repeated startup attempts if damage is apparent
3. Arrange manufacturer assessment
Verify: Technician confirms safe startup
Escalate: Visible damage or liquid exposure requires service
Source: https://www.dell.com/support/kbdoc/en-us/000141584/how-to-troubleshoot-a-no-post-issue-on-a-dell-notebook-pc
