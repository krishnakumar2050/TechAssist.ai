# Laptop overheats and shuts down

ID: TA-LAP-003 | Platform: Dell laptops | Category: hardware / cooling

Symptom: Heat and unexpected shutdown under load
Possible causes (not confirmed): Restricted cooling or fan fault
Resolution:
1. Use a hard ventilated surface
2. Reduce workload
3. Run model-supported preboot fan diagnostics
Verify: Normal workload completes without shutdown
Escalate: Persistent overheating or failed fan diagnostic requires service
Source: https://www.dell.com/support/kbdoc/en-us/000130867/how-to-troubleshoot-a-overheating-shutdown-or-thermal-issue-on-a-dell-pc
