# Laptop chassis or trackpad is bulging

ID: TA-LAP-005 | Platform: Dell laptops | Category: hardware / battery_safety

Symptom: Swelling or lifted keyboard or trackpad
Possible causes (not confirmed): Possible battery fault
Resolution:
1. Power off
2. Disconnect external power if safe
3. Stop using the device
4. Contact authorized service without opening or pressing the battery
Verify: Authorized technician assesses and resolves fault
Escalate: Escalate immediately; do not continue normal troubleshooting
Source: https://www.dell.com/support/kbdoc/en-us/000130867/how-to-troubleshoot-a-overheating-shutdown-or-thermal-issue-on-a-dell-pc
