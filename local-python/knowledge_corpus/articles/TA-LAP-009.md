# Windows camera is black or missing

ID: TA-LAP-009 | Platform: Windows 11 | Category: hardware / camera

Symptom: No preview or camera unavailable
Possible causes (not confirmed): Obstruction or driver or hardware fault
Resolution:
1. Check privacy shutter
2. Restart
3. Test Camera app
4. Check camera device and approved driver updates
Verify: Camera preview returns
Escalate: Camera missing after supported checks requires service
Source: https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/hardware/camera/camera-doesn-t-work-in-windows
