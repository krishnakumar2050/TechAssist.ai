# Bluetooth audio disconnects repeatedly

ID: TA-LAP-016 | Platform: Windows 11 | Category: software / bluetooth_audio

Symptom: Intermittent wireless playback
Possible causes (not confirmed): Driver or connection compatibility issue
Resolution:
1. Toggle Bluetooth
2. Pair the headset again
3. Check approved Bluetooth drivers and Windows updates
Verify: Playback remains connected during a test call
Escalate: Collect adapter and headset models for support
