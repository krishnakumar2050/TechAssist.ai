# MacBook battery will not charge

ID: TA-LAP-023 | Platform: macOS; Mac laptops | Category: hardware / charging

Symptom: Charging does not start
Possible causes (not confirmed): Adapter or battery or other hardware issue
Resolution:
1. Check compatible power adapter and cable
2. Inspect for damage
3. Use Apple Diagnostics guidance for the model
Verify: Charging status and battery level increase normally
Escalate: Damaged power accessories or persistent failure require Apple service
Source: https://support.apple.com/en-gb/guide/mac-help/-mh29198/mac

Reviewed: 2026-11-03. Original editorial summary; not lab tested.
